Start creating

Choose Draw for a blank grid. Starter designs contains 20 editable designs: 10 are free and 10 require Pro. Each selection creates an independent copy. Pro also provides the object library, including 64 original colored assets organized by themes, seasons and activities. You can preview locked content before purchasing. Objects already placed in artwork remain editable without Pro.

Drawing, built-in colors, layers, shapes, selections, animation, Undo and Redo remain available without Pro. Existing palettes remain usable. Pro adds palette management, photo and camera conversion, Image Playground import, named-project saving and import, iCloud workspaces, and output delivery. Image and paper previews remain available without Pro.

Canvas space and navigation

Drawing tools remain visible independently of the other editor panels. Use the tools bar's collapse button to hide or reopen it, and drag its handle to reposition it. It starts along the bottom in portrait and at the right in landscape; scroll the tools to reach every tool. Other panels start collapsed. Use their chevron to reveal layers, timeline and editing controls.

Spread two fingers to zoom in, pinch to zoom out and drag with two fingers to move. Canvas navigation offers Fit canvas, Fit artwork, Focus object and 100% physical-pixel view. Focus object requires a selection. Navigation does not change artwork or add Undo steps. Use one finger to draw; object move/rotate mode changes what that gesture does, so check the mode indicator.

Reference and actual-size preview

Open Reference & 1× preview in the editor. On iPad it can sit beside the canvas; on a compact screen it opens as a sheet. The actual-size artwork preview is available without Pro. Choosing a reference image requires Pro. Reference images stay in the current editor session, are cleared when you close the artwork, and are not included in saved projects or exports.

Linked cels in animation

Expand the editor panels, open Timeline, then choose Edit frame range and choose a source frame plus destination frames on the active layer. Link cels shares pixel content across those frames; timing, source-image settings, masks and object membership remain local to each frame. Preview the change before accepting replacement of destination artwork. Cancel leaves the artwork unchanged.

Editing linked pixels updates their linked peers on the same layer. Making a visible pixel transparent can remove its object membership in each affected frame; explicitly owned transparent pixels remain owned. The chain indicator shows how many cels share the active cel’s pixels. Unlink creates independent cels with identical pixels. Link and Unlink each have one Undo step. Frame copy and duplicate operations remain independent. Hidden or locked layers, including layers within hidden or locked groups, must be made editable before linking or editing their cels. Linked cels remain available without Pro. Unlink affected cels before merging linked layers. A genuine image replacement makes only its target cel independent; the import preview explains that change.

Transparency and Pixel Inspector

The light checkerboard represents transparency. Erasing removes a pixel's color. PNG can preserve transparency or use a white background selected in Preview & Export.

Pixel Inspector lets you choose an exact row and column, inspect its color, and paint, erase or pick that color. These controls also support precise edits with VoiceOver.

Revisiting a photo conversion

Projects with retained source images can show their saved conversion in Artwork actions > Edit conversion. Changing and applying conversion requires Pro. Preview before applying or Cancel. For indexed artwork, confirm palette matching separately from replacement; changed settings reset approval. Other layers and frames remain intact, and an applied conversion has one Undo.

An older project may contain only pixels. Keep editing those pixels even if its original image is unavailable. Importing a new source requires Pro.

Private recovery and named projects

Drawing changes are protected in private recovery on your device without Pro. Done returns to the library after protecting current changes; it does not by itself promise a named-project save. Check the status and any storage error before closing. Private recoveries remain available to reopen and edit if Pro expires or purchase verification is unavailable.

Saving a named project, Save Copy, project import and iCloud workspace actions require verified Pro access. Save Copy creates an independent named project from a recovery. It does not replace the original. If verification is unavailable, retry verification and then choose the action again. Restoring purchases does not automatically resume an interrupted action.

Optional iCloud workspaces use your Apple Account and Apple's storage service. A protected private recovery does not mean the original file or iCloud project was updated: check the stated destination and publication status. Opening an existing project stages a private local recovery before editing. A conflict or failed publication should keep that recovery and its source images. Review conflicting versions before choosing a replacement; keep the files if the provider is unavailable. TelaPixel does not operate its own project-storage server or account-recovery service.

Exporting and printing

Preview & Export shows PNG enlargement/background and paper size, orientation, margins and placement. Pro is required to save or share PNG, export editable projects or advanced animation/game assets, create PDF and use AirPrint. Preview dimensions and layout before delivery. A compatible printer determines the final paper and printable margins.

Editable-project exports include saved source images when present. Share PNG or PDF when you want to send only rendered artwork. Keep a separate exported editable backup somewhere you control. Photos, Files, sharing apps, printers and any cloud services you select manage their copies separately.

If Photos permission was declined, change it in Settings or use another available output destination. Canceling a later action does not remove files already delivered.

Pro purchases

Pro is an optional monthly subscription. The purchase screen shows the localized price and renewal terms. Manage cancellation in your Apple Account subscription settings; Restore purchases is available in the app. Recognized, verified legacy lifetime purchases retain Pro access. Purchase cancellation, a pending purchase or unavailable verification must not change your artwork.

AI Aid and Image Playground

AI Aid requires Pro and appears only when Apple's native Image Playground is available. Availability and generation are governed by Apple Intelligence and your device settings, including software, language and region. Requests may use Apple's cloud; TelaPixel has no image-generation backend. Accepted images are converted to pixels locally. Ordinary drawing and photo conversion processing do not require Apple Intelligence or a TelaPixel account.

If generation stalls, cancel and retry with a short subject and setting. Report the device, system, language and whether Cancel remained responsive. Avoid sending private photos or personal suggestions unless needed to explain the issue.

Language and accessibility

TelaPixel supports English, Italian, French and Spanish through the device or per-app language setting. Report clipped text, unlabeled controls or VoiceOver problems, including your text-size setting.

A missing original or damaged project

Pixel artwork may remain editable when a source image is missing. If an earlier saved snapshot is recovered, the app warns that recent edits may be absent. Keep existing files and report the exact error. Do not delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot artwork you have not backed up: private recoveries exist on the device, and removing the app can remove them.

What to include in a report

  • Device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version/build and language.
  • Steps, expected result and actual result, including whether it repeats.
  • Whether you were drawing, navigating, moving an object or publishing a project.
  • A screenshot or short recording with personal information removed.
