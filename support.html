Start creating

Choose Draw for a blank grid, Starter designs for an editable copy of one of the ten examples, or Photo to convert an image. The camera option is in the library's additional creation menu.

Use the pencil, eraser and color picker to refine your artwork. Select an area to cut, copy, move, crop or rotate it. Undo and Redo help you try changes without losing your previous step.

Zooming and moving the canvas

Spread two fingers to zoom in, bring them together to zoom out, and drag with two fingers to move around. Fit canvas returns the artwork to a useful overview. Use one finger to draw.

If a gesture behaves unexpectedly, include whether you were zooming, panning or drawing when it happened, and the approximate canvas size.

Why erased pixels show a light pattern

The white and light-gray pattern represents transparency. Erasing removes the pixel's color. You can preserve that transparency in a PNG or select White background in Preview & Export for a solid white background.

What Pixel Inspector does

Pixel Inspector lets you choose an exact row and column, inspect that pixel's color, and paint, erase or pick its color. It is useful for precise corrections and provides controls that can be used with VoiceOver.

Revisiting a photo conversion

Imported projects retain their source image and conversion settings. Open the saved artwork, open Artwork actions and choose Edit conversion. Adjust the preview, then apply it or Cancel. Applying the conversion replaces the current canvas and can be undone in the editor.

Projects made before source-image retention was introduced may contain only pixel artwork. For those projects, you can keep editing the pixels or import the original photo again.

Saving and sharing

Use Done to return to the library after editing. Check the save status and any error message before closing the app if storage is low or a save has failed.

Preview & Export lets you save a PNG to Photos or Files, share an image, prepare a PDF or export an editable project. PNG enlargement keeps pixel edges crisp. Image and paper preferences are retained with the project.

Editable-project exports include the saved source image. Use PNG or PDF when you want to share only the rendered artwork. Keep an exported editable copy somewhere you control if you want a separate backup. The app does not provide a TelaPixel cloud backup.

If Photos permission was declined, you can change it in Settings or use Files instead.

Printing

In paper preview, choose paper size, orientation, margins and placement. Fit keeps the entire design visible; Fill may crop its edges; Exact size uses the dimensions you enter. Check the stated artwork dimensions and then the final AirPrint preview.

A compatible printer is required. The printer may substitute its available paper or printable margins, so the final print can differ from the app's initial layout.

AI Aid and Image Playground

AI Aid appears only when Apple's Image Playground is available. Compatible hardware alone does not guarantee that it is ready: Apple Intelligence settings, software, language, region and model availability can affect it.

Image Playground is Apple's generation interface. Depending on the system version and request, it may use Apple's cloud. Ordinary drawing and photo conversion remain local and do not need Apple Intelligence.

If items cannot be added or generation does not progress, try a short subject and setting, such as a red mushroom in green grass. If Cancel still works, close Image Playground and reopen it. When reporting the problem, include the exact items or prompt, the device model, OS version, language, and whether Cancel remained responsive. Do not include personal people suggestions or private photos unless they are necessary to explain the issue.

Language and accessibility

TelaPixel follows the device or per-app language preference and supports English, Italian, French and Spanish. The editor also provides labeled controls and Pixel Inspector for exact-cell actions. Please report any clipped text, unlabeled control or VoiceOver difficulty, including your text-size setting.

A missing original or damaged project

If the original image is missing, the pixel artwork can remain editable even when reconversion is unavailable. If the app recovers an earlier saved version, it tells you that recent edits may be missing.

Keep the existing files while asking for help. Deleting a project also removes its local source images and recovery copies. Send the exact error first; do not delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot a project you have not backed up.

What to include in a report

  • Device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • TelaPixel version/build from TestFlight, if applicable.
  • Steps that led to the issue and what you expected.
  • Whether it happens every time.
  • A screenshot or short recording when helpful.

Remove personal information from attachments. The privacy policy explains local storage, exported originals and optional Apple services.
