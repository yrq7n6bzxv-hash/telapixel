Your artwork and photos

TelaPixel stores your pixel artwork, project names, imported source images, conversion settings and output preferences on your device. It uses this information to display, edit, save, reopen and export your projects. Ordinary drawing, photo conversion and the available local image effects run on the device.

When you import a photo, capture one or accept a generated image, the app can keep the source image with the saved project so you can change the conversion later. That copy may include metadata present in the imported image. TelaPixel does not automatically upload your project library to a server operated by the developer. It does not require a TelaPixel account.

The app may keep earlier source images and a previous project snapshot so that Undo and recovery can work. Deleting a project from the library also removes its associated local source and recovery files. Previously exported images, project files, device backups and copies held by other people are managed separately.

Photos, camera, Files and printing

You choose which images to import through Apple's photo picker. Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo. Permission to add images to Photos is requested when you save artwork there.

Saving to Files, sharing and AirPrint use system interfaces. The destination you select receives the file or print content. Photos, iCloud Drive, another file provider, a sharing app or a printer may handle that content under its own settings and policies.

An exported editable project includes its saved source image when one is present. Someone you share that project with can access the original, not just the pixel version. PNG and PDF exports contain the rendered artwork instead of the editable project and its source image.

You can change app permissions in your device's Settings. Declining photo or camera access does not prevent you from drawing or using the built-in starter designs.

Optional Apple Intelligence features

AI Aid opens Apple's native Image Playground only when your device reports that it is available. When you choose to use it, Apple handles the generation experience and its account, language and availability settings. Your request may be processed using Apple's cloud services. TelaPixel does not provide a separate AI account, operate an image-generation server or offer a ChatGPT/external-provider option within this integration.

After you accept an image, TelaPixel receives the resulting image and converts it to pixels locally. The separate style-description helper, when available, uses Apple's on-device language model to choose a conversion preset.

Apple explains its handling of these requests in Apple Intelligence & Privacy. Apple describes Private Cloud Compute as processing requests for the requested task without retaining their content after completion; its notice also describes Apple's operational information and optional analytics. Your device's Apple settings govern these services.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated usage-tracking service. TelaPixel does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Apple may provide platform services and diagnostics according to your device settings and its policies. This is separate from a TelaPixel-operated analytics service.

TestFlight and support

If you join the beta through TestFlight, Apple provides beta distribution, testing information and feedback tools. The developer can receive tester information, device and version details, session or crash information, and feedback or screenshots you submit. Invitation details may include the name and email address used to invite you. This information is used to run the beta, investigate problems and improve the app. See Apple's TestFlight information.

If you email support, the developer receives your address and whatever text or attachments you choose to send. That information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Share only what is needed, especially when attaching photos or editable projects.

Support correspondence and issue records are retained while needed to handle the request and related follow-up. Contact the developer to ask about access to or deletion of information you have sent. Apple manages its own TestFlight and service records under its policies. The developer cannot retrieve or delete projects that exist only on your device.

Your choices

You can use drawing and ordinary conversion without AI Aid. You decide which photos to import, whether to grant camera or Photos permissions, what to share, and whether to send support or beta feedback.

Delete unwanted projects in TelaPixel and remove exported copies from their destinations separately. Device backups and cloud services you enable may retain copies according to their own settings. TelaPixel does not offer its own cloud backup or account-recovery service.

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. Effective date: 5 September 2026.
